# Product Principles

Walking Dog exists to make dogs and owners happier through better walks. Product
decisions are judged against three axes.

## Dog Experience

The feature should deepen dog-to-dog encounters or the relationship context around
those encounters.

Review question: does this make dog meetings, pack context, or shared walks more
meaningful for the dog?

## Data-Maximized Walks

The feature should capture, preserve, or explain walk data in a way that can improve
future walk experiences.

Review question: does this data help improve routes, duration, timing, events,
goals, or post-walk understanding?

## Owner Contribution

The feature should help owners see that their dog enjoyed the walk and feel motivated
to contribute again.

Review question: does this screen or workflow make the owner think, "I want to walk
my dog more"?

## Evidence Standard

Do not claim product fit from intent alone. A pull request should attach evidence:

- Screen or flow proof for the affected journey.
- Data proof that the right record was created, updated, or read.
- Error-path proof when the journey can fail.
- A short note naming which product axis improved and why.
